The four things that stop people

After a decade of first sessions, the same four things come up. None of them is a good reason not to start.

“I have not got time”

A session is an hour, three times a week, at a time you choose. Open 6am–9pm, every day. If the timetable does not work around your life, tell us and we will be honest about whether we can fit you in.

“I cannot stay consistent”

Which is why the sessions are booked, coached and expected. Consistency is much easier when somebody notices you are missing.

“I will feel watched”

Five people, one coach, everyone concentrating on their own work. Nobody is looking at you — they are busy.

“I do not know what to eat”

We cover that too, with practical guidance rather than a meal plan to buy. Start with training; food follows more easily once you are moving.
