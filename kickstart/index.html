Week 1 — finding your feet

Just keep showing up.

Three coached sessions, never more than five of you to a coach. Learn the movements, meet the others, get comfortable in the space — consistency beats intensity, every single time.

coach’s note: your programme’s already written. All you need to bring is yourself.

Week 2 — proper coaching, every session

Personal training, in good company.

Your coach knows your name, your goals and your numbers — guiding your technique and adapting every exercise to suit you. No wandering around a gym floor, no generic workouts, and plenty of encouragement from the four training beside you.

Week 3 — habits that stick

The plan follows you home.

No crash diets and no fads — just simple, sustainable habits built step by step, with a weekly catch-up with your coach to keep everything ticking along.

coach’s note: one habit at a time. That’s how they stick.
