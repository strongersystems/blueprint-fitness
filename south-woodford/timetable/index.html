What the sessions are

Know before you go

SGPT

Small group personal training, with max 6 per coach.

Sweat

Conditioning: heart rate up, held there.

Metcon

Metabolic conditioning with mixed-modal work.

Barbell Club

Technical barbell work: squat, press, pull.

Recover

Mobility, breathing and soft-tissue work.

Boxing

Pad work, footwork and conditioning.

Tough Guy

The hard one, so bring a towel.
