Bookings & cancellations

All bookings and cancellations are managed through the Blueprint app, which every member has access to.

Booking sessions

  • SGPT sessions have up to 8 spaces, depending on coach availability.
  • TEAM sessions have 12–15 spaces, depending on the class.
  • You can book sessions up to 2 weeks in advance.
  • Bookings close 1 hour before the session starts.

Trying to book within an hour of the session? Simply message the coach running the session or post in the relevant WhatsApp group — we’ll manually add you if there’s space.

Waitlists

Every session has an unlimited waitlist. If a space becomes available:

  • You’ll be notified via the app and/or email.
  • You have 2 hours to accept the space before it’s offered to the next person in the queue.

Please make sure app notifications are switched ON and keep an eye on your emails. Members will often (although they’re not required to) post in the WhatsApp group when they’ve cancelled a session, giving those on the waitlist a heads-up.

Ultimately, it’s your responsibility to monitor the waitlist and claim any place that becomes available.

If you’re on a waitlist, it’s worth being prepared to train at short notice. Last-minute cancellations happen regularly, and members who are ready to go often benefit from them.

If you know you can no longer make the session, please remove yourself from the waitlist so someone else has the opportunity.

Cancellations

We operate a 9-hour cancellation policy, which was chosen by members to give everyone a fair opportunity to use available spaces.

  • If you cancel within 9 hours of your session, you will lose that booking.
  • If there are genuine mitigating circumstances, please contact your coach, Paul or Stu directly.

Swapping sessions

Changing from one session to another within the 9-hour cancellation window is still treated as a cancellation (unless you’re moving to an earlier session).

For example: “I can’t make the 7am, can I swap to the 5pm?” sent at 6:30am is still a late cancellation.

Nightly blackout period

The system pauses both the cancellation countdown and waitlist expiry overnight.

  • Blackout hours: 11:00pm – 5:00am

Example

With a 9-hour cancellation policy:

  • For a 7:00am session, the cancellation deadline is 4:00pm the previous day.
  • Waitlist offers do not expire overnight, giving members until after 5:00am to claim any spaces released during the night.

If you think the blackout period has caused you to unfairly lose a session, send us a message. We’ll check it and reinstate the booking if appropriate.

WhatsApp etiquette

If you cancel at short notice and the session has a waitlist, it’s good etiquette to post a quick message in the WhatsApp group so anyone waiting knows a space may now be available.

If:

  • there is no waitlist, or
  • you’re cancelling several days in advance,

there’s no need to post anything.

You also don’t need to explain why you’re cancelling — this isn’t school!

Unsure if you can make it?

If you’re not certain you’ll be able to attend, it’s often better not to book until you’re sure.

Only a handful of sessions regularly fill up, so booking closer to the time usually isn’t a problem and helps avoid unnecessary late cancellations.

TEAM sessions

Although TEAM classes are unlimited, every unused place is still a wasted opportunity for another member. The same booking and cancellation rules apply.

Members who repeatedly book TEAM sessions and fail to attend or cancel late may receive a three-strikes warning, after which they’ll lose the ability to book TEAM sessions for one week.

If you’re ever unsure about any of the above, please get in touch. We’d much rather answer a question than have someone lose a booking unnecessarily.
