- Founders Trial price Hackney: **£129** (was £169), + **15% off ongoing memberships for life**
- Includes: InBody scan + goal-setting onboarding; 3x coached small-group sessions/week (ldn: 12 personal training sessions); larger group conditioning sessions / unlimited classes; weekly 1-to-1 accountability check-ins with Head Coach Nick; nutrition guidance; beginners' intro session; priority pre-opening access.
- Process (4 steps, numbered 01–04 on their site): The Onboarding → Small-Group Training → Habit Stacking → Guaranteed Results ("build muscle, lose fat, and feel more confident in 30 days").
