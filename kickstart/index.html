Your first month

Your 30 days,
drawn to scale

We believe in habit stacking, which means getting one simple habit down each week before adding the next.

You are here

01 / 30

Every box is a day trained with us, coached and planned.

D1

Day one: welcome in

Walk in and we’ll take care of the rest.

A proper welcome, an InBody scan and a friendly chat about your goals. We’ll get to know you, understand where you’d like to be, and map out how we’ll get there together.

coach’s note: everyone’s a bit nervous on day one, and it soon passes.
W1

Week 1: finding your feet

Just keep showing up.

Three coached sessions, with never more than five or six of you to a coach, while you learn the movements, meet the others and get comfortable in the space. Consistency beats intensity, every time.

coach’s note: your programme’s already written, so all you need to bring is yourself.
W2

Week 2: proper coaching, every session

Personal training, in good company.

Your coach knows your name, your goals and your numbers, and adapts every exercise to suit you, with plenty of encouragement from the four training beside you.

coach’s note: we track your weights, so you never have to guess.
W3

Week 3: habits that stick

The plan follows you home.

Simple habits you can keep, built one step at a time, with a weekly catch-up with your coach to keep everything ticking along.

coach’s note: one habit at a time is how they stick.
D30

Day 30: look how far you’ve come

Stronger, fitter, and sure of yourself.

We sit down together, remeasure, and celebrate what’s changed. If you’d like to stay, your next 12-week block is planned the same way, with your coach and your crew behind you.

coach’s note: this is the bit where most people say they wish they’d started sooner.
