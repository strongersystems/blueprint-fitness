Privacy policy

What we collect when you enquire, sign up or train with us, and what we do with it.

This policy covers blueprintfitnessldn.com and the three Blueprint Fitness studios. The data controller is PRS Coaching Ltd, trading as Blueprint Fitness, registered in England & Wales.

1. Who we are

Blueprint Fitness is a small group personal training business with three studios in East London. PRS Coaching Ltd is responsible for the personal information described here.

For anything about your data, whether it’s a question, a correction or a request to delete it, email info@blueprintfitnessldn.com. If it is specific to one studio you can email that studio directly:

  • South Woodford: southwoodford@blueprintfitnessldn.com
  • Leytonstone: leytonstone@blueprintfitnessldn.com
  • Hackney: hackney@blueprintfitnessldn.com

2. What we collect

2.1 When you get in touch or enquire

The forms on this site (the enquiry form, the 30-Day Kickstart sign-up, the nutrition consultation request, the member check-in and the cancellation request) ask for some or all of: your first name, email address, phone number, which studio you are interested in, and whatever you write in the message box.

2.2 When you become a member

We hold the details needed to run your membership: your contact details, your chosen membership and start date, your payment details and payment history, your attendance and bookings, and the notes your coach keeps on your training.

2.3 Health information

If you tell us about an injury, a condition or anything else that affects how you should train, we record it so your coach can keep you safe. This is health data, which the law treats as a special category. We only use it to deliver your training safely, we do not use it for marketing, and we never share it outside the coaching team without your permission.

2.4 When you use the website

Our host keeps standard server logs, which include IP addresses, for security and to keep the site running. Our CRM sets an identifier in your browser so that if you enquire later we can tell which page or advert brought you here (see section 5).

2.5 Photos and video

We sometimes photograph or film in the studios for social media and the website. We ask first, and you can say no, or change your mind later and ask us to take something down.

3. Why we use it

Under UK data protection law we need a lawful basis for everything we do with your information, and ours are:

  • To run your membership: taking payment, booking you into sessions, programming your training, contacting you about your membership. The basis is that we have a contract with you.
  • To reply to you: answering an enquiry, arranging a taster session, following up after you have been in. The basis is our legitimate interest in responding to someone who has asked to hear from us.
  • To train you safely: using the health information you give us. The basis is your explicit consent, which you give by telling us, and you can withdraw it at any time.
  • To send you marketing: emails and texts about offers, events and the Kickstart. The basis is your consent, and every message has an unsubscribe link. Stopping marketing does not affect the emails we have to send you about your membership.
  • To keep our accounts: the basis is our legal obligation to retain financial records.

4. Who else sees it

We do not sell your information, and we do not share it for anyone else's marketing. We do use these companies to run the business, and they process data on our instructions:

  • GoHighLevel: our CRM. It powers every form on this site, stores your contact record, and sends our emails and texts.
  • Our payment provider: membership payments are taken through the sign-up forms. Your card details go straight to the payment provider; we never see or store a full card number.
  • TeamUp: the booking system at South Woodford and Leytonstone, which holds your bookings and attendance.
  • Google: the fonts and the studio maps on this site are served by Google, so Google receives your IP address when a page loads.
  • YouTube: the nutrition videos in the members' area are hosted on YouTube in its no-cookie mode, so nothing is set unless you press play.
  • GitHub: hosts this website.

If you message us on WhatsApp, that conversation is on Meta's platform and subject to their privacy policy as well as ours.

We will also share information where the law requires it, for example with HMRC, or if we are ever obliged to by a court.

Some of these companies are based outside the UK. Where that is the case, the transfer is covered by the safeguards UK data protection law requires.

5. Cookies and tracking

This site does not use advertising cookies and there is no analytics suite tracking you around it. Two things do store something in your browser:

  • Our CRM's tracking script, which runs on every page and sets an identifier so that an enquiry can be matched to the page or advert you arrived from.
  • Embedded content: the Google Maps on the studio pages, and the YouTube videos in the members' area if you play one. These set their own cookies, which are governed by Google's privacy policy rather than this one.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Doing so will not stop you using the site, though the maps and videos may not work.

6. How long we keep it

  • Enquiries that do not become memberships: kept for up to 24 months, then deleted.
  • Member records: kept while you are a member and for 24 months afterwards, so that rejoining is simple.
  • Financial records: kept for six years after the end of the relevant tax year, because we are legally required to.
  • Health information: deleted when you stop training with us, unless you ask us to keep it for a future return.

You can ask us to delete your information sooner than any of the above, and we will unless the law requires us to keep it.

7. Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to:

  • ask for a copy of the information we hold about you;
  • have anything inaccurate corrected;
  • have your information deleted;
  • object to how we are using it, or ask us to restrict its use;
  • withdraw consent at any time, for marketing or for anything else you consented to;
  • ask us to send your information to you or another provider in a portable format.

Email info@blueprintfitnessldn.com and we will respond within one month, free of charge.

If you are not happy with how we have handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113. We would rather you came to us first so we can put it right.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change how we use your information we will update this page and change the date at the top. If the change is significant we will tell members directly rather than rely on you checking.
