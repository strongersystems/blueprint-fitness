Membership terms & conditions

This agreement is between you (“the Member”) and PRS Coaching Ltd trading as Blueprint Fitness (“Blueprint Fitness”, “we”, “us”).

1. General

1.1 Membership payments

Membership fees are payable in advance. Your membership will not be activated until payment has been received.

1.2 Exercise risk

Exercise carries an inherent level of risk. By participating in our services, you acknowledge and accept these risks. Blueprint Fitness and its coaches will not be liable for any injury sustained whilst participating in training, except where caused by our negligence or a deliberate act.

1.3 Following coaching instructions

Members agree to follow all reasonable instructions given by Blueprint Fitness coaches and staff to help ensure a safe and effective training environment.

1.4 Your responsibility

Results depend on many factors including your attendance, consistency, effort, nutrition, recovery and adherence to the programme both inside and outside the gym. Whilst we’ll provide every tool possible to help you succeed, we cannot guarantee specific results.

1.5 Membership use

Your membership grants access only to the services included within your chosen membership package and is subject to availability and our booking policies.

1.6 Health declaration

By becoming a member you confirm that:

  • you are medically fit to exercise; or
  • you have sought appropriate medical advice where necessary.

If your health changes during your membership, you agree to inform us before continuing to train.

Any assessments carried out by Blueprint Fitness (including InBody scans or movement screens) are designed to monitor progress and are not intended as medical or diagnostic assessments.

2. Membership term

2.1 Minimum commitment

Your membership includes a minimum commitment period of three (3) months, starting from your membership start date.

2.2 Rolling membership

After your initial commitment has ended, your membership automatically continues on a rolling monthly basis until cancelled in accordance with Section 5.

2.3 Price changes

Your membership price is fixed during your initial commitment period. Should membership prices change in future, we will provide at least 30 days’ notice before any increase takes effect.

2.4 Cooling-off period

You may cancel your membership within 7 days of joining for a full refund, provided you have not substantially used the services during that period where applicable.

3. Liability

3.1 Damage to property

Members are responsible for any damage they deliberately or negligently cause to Blueprint Fitness property or equipment.

3.2 Use of facilities

Members use the facilities and equipment at their own risk and should only use equipment they understand how to operate safely.

3.3 Personal belongings

Blueprint Fitness accepts no responsibility for the loss, theft or damage of personal belongings. Lost property will normally be retained for 14 days before being disposed of or donated.

3.4 Member conduct

We reserve the right to suspend or terminate any membership without refund where a member:

  • behaves aggressively or abusively;
  • puts others at risk;
  • repeatedly ignores coach instructions;
  • damages equipment;
  • or otherwise breaches our Code of Conduct.

4. Membership

4.1 Membership payments

Memberships are collected monthly via recurring card payment or Direct Debit through our payment provider.

4.2 Session allowance

Your membership includes the number of SGPT sessions included within your chosen membership, together with access to any TEAM sessions included in your package. Unused sessions do not roll over into future months.

4.3 Automatic renewal

Following the initial three-month commitment, memberships continue automatically on a rolling monthly basis until cancelled by either party in accordance with these Terms.

4.4 Payment authority

You confirm that you are authorised to use the payment method registered against your membership.

4.5 Failed payments

If payments remain outstanding for more than 28 days, Blueprint Fitness reserves the right to suspend or terminate your membership until your account is brought up to date.

4.6 Age restrictions

  • Members must be aged 18 or over.
  • Members aged 16–17 may train with written parental consent.
  • Members under 16 must be supervised at all times unless otherwise agreed by Blueprint Fitness.

4.7 Memberships are personal

Memberships are personal to the named member and cannot be shared, transferred or assigned.

5. Cancellations

Please read this section carefully.

5.1 During your initial commitment

If you wish to cancel during your initial three-month commitment, you must provide 30 days’ written notice.

5.2 After your initial commitment

Once your membership has moved onto a rolling monthly agreement, you must provide 30 days’ written notice to cancel. Where appropriate, any payment covering days beyond your final membership date will be prorated.

5.3 Early termination

If you choose to end your membership before completing your initial three-month commitment, an early termination fee equal to 50% of the remaining membership fees will apply. The 30-day notice period still applies.

5.4 Upfront memberships

Memberships paid fully in advance are non-refundable.

5.5 Your responsibility

Members are responsible for ensuring their cancellation request has been submitted successfully. Not receiving reminder emails does not remove this responsibility.

5.6 Outstanding payments

Outstanding balances remain payable. Blueprint Fitness reserves the right to recover unpaid debts through a third-party collection agency where necessary. Any additional recovery costs may be added to the outstanding balance where permitted by law.

5.7 Exceptional circumstances

We may agree to cancel your membership early if:

  • you permanently move more than 10 miles from any Blueprint Fitness location (proof of address required); or
  • you are unable to continue exercising due to a medical condition (medical evidence required).

Any such requests are considered at Management’s discretion.

6. Our commitment

We take enormous pride in delivering a coaching service that is professional, structured and genuinely invested in helping our members succeed. In return, we ask that members honour the commitment they’ve chosen when joining Blueprint Fitness.

These terms allow us to continue investing in our coaches, facilities and community whilst providing the high standard of service our members expect.

If you have any questions about these Terms & Conditions before joining, please ask — we’re always happy to explain anything that’s unclear.
