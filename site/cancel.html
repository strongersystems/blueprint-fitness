Cancellations & paused memberships

Let us know you need to make changes

Life changes — memberships can too. Here’s how changes, pauses and cancellations work, and exactly what to do for each.

Changes & pauses

Need to adjust, not cancel?

Most changes just need an email before your next billing cycle date so there’s time to process them.

Moving up or down

Change your subscription

We can change your membership subscription at any time. We’ll need to be notified in writing before your next billing cycle date in order for it to be processed in sufficient time.

Email us to request a change

Taking a breather

Pause your membership

We’re able to pause for one month at a time. After the paused month, your membership reinstates and the monthly fee is taken as normal.

Email us to request a pause

Off on holiday?

Holiday adjustments

We can’t pause for two weeks within a billing cycle — instead, reduce your subscription to suit the sessions you need that month (for example, drop from SGPT 12 to SGPT 8), then move back when you return.

Email us about a holiday

For any membership change, we need to be notified before your next billing cycle date in order for it to be processed in sufficient time.
