What we do

Personal training.
In a small group.

This is not a conventional gym where you pay for access and are left to figure everything out alone.

01

Small Group Personal Training

Strength training is at the heart of everything we do. Dynamic coach-led sessions in groups of no more than five people per coach — every workout has a purpose, every programme is tailored to your goals.

02

Team Training

Build a stronger heart, healthier lungs and a better engine through larger group cardio and conditioning sessions — designed to complement your strength programme.

Nutrition Coaching

No meal plans. No fad diets. Just practical nutrition coaching from our in-house team, helping you build simple, sustainable habits that fit around your life.
